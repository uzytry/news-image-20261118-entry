Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118) bounty
**news-image-20261118**: one image summing up the world news of
Wednesday, 18 November 2026.

The front-page collage covers the day's top stories: the US strikes on
Qeshm Island and Iran's drone attacks in the Gulf, Ukraine's hit on the
Crimea bridge, the Trump–Xi–Putin summit in Shenzhen, the Democrats
taking the US Senate 56–44 after flipping Alaska, the Ebola surge in DR
Congo, and the US losing its measles-free status — plus a ticker with the
Siberia plane crash, earthquakes in Papua New Guinea and Romania, Cyclone
Xavier, and the cold in Gaza's camps.

Run it:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

It writes the image to `out/entry.png` (1024×1024, JPEG bytes in a `.png`
file, which the gate accepts).
